# Design Critique & Anti-Template Evaluation Protocol

Before shipping any page, run this 6-point Art Director Critique:

1. **The Squint Test (Silhouette & Rhythm):**
   - If you squint at the page from 2 meters away, do you see a monotonous stack of rectangular boxes with identical headers, or do you see dramatic shifts in scale, open negative space, and varied visual compositions?
2. **Hero Originality Check:**
   - Is the hero a generic 2-column split with pill badges and 2 rectangular buttons? If yes, reject and redesign as an editorial title page or typographic composition.
3. **Project Individuality Check:**
   - Are all 3 projects rendered inside the exact same card loop? If yes, redesign so each project's layout reflects its medium (Web Platform vs. Mobile Utility vs. Indie RPG).
4. **Icon & Badge Purge:**
   - Count how many decorative icons (`Lucide`) and bordered pill badges appear on screen. Strip 80% of them. Let typography, scale, and layout carry hierarchy.
5. **Dual-Discipline Harmony:**
   - Do Full-Stack Engineering and Game Development feel integrated through shared craft (interactive systems, state responsiveness, narrative pacing) rather than separated into two generic icon boxes?
6. **Bilingual & Responsive Integrity:**
   - Verify both `/en` and `/id` read naturally as native editorial prose, and ensure mobile layouts feel like a pocket art publication rather than compressed desktop tables.
